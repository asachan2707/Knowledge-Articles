8 Cache Eviction Strategies

LRU, LFU, FIFO, TTL and others: the logic and the best fit for each.

2 min read · topic 4 of 5 in Caching
StrategyLogicBest for
LRUEvict least recently accessedGeneral purpose (most common)
MRUEvict most recently used firstWhen recent items unlikely to be re-read
LFUEvict lowest access frequencyLong-lived caches, popularity-skewed data
FIFOEvict oldest-inserted item firstSimple queues, fairness over recency
SLRUTwo segments: probationary → protectedAvoid one-time scan polluting hot cache
TTLFixed lifespan per itemSession data, tokens, OTPs
Two-TieredIn-memory L1 + distributed L2High-performance apps needing failover
Random ReplacementEvict random itemSimple, when access pattern is truly random
