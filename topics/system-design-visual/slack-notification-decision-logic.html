Slack — Notification Decision Logic

The rules behind whether a Slack message notifies you, and why it is complex.

2 min read · topic 5 of 9 in Real-World

Slack's notification system is a production example of a simple feature hiding extraordinary complexity. Before sending a notification, Slack evaluates: user's notification settings, DND schedule, channel mute status, mention type (@channel vs @here vs direct), user's current online status across devices, recent activity (suppress if user already saw the message), and platform-specific payload limits. The flowchart for this decision has 20+ branches — a reminder that "send notification" is never a one-liner in production.
