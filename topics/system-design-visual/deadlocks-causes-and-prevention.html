Deadlocks — Causes & Prevention

The four Coffman conditions and the practical ways to avoid deadlocks.

2 min read · topic 6 of 7 in Databases

Coffman Conditions (all four must hold simultaneously for a deadlock): Mutual exclusion · Hold and wait · No preemption · Circular wait.

Prevention

  • Resource ordering — acquire locks in a fixed order
  • Timeouts — roll back long-held transactions
  • Banker's Algorithm — simulate before granting

Recovery

  • DBMS detects cycle in wait-for graph
  • Selects a victim (lowest cost transaction)
  • Rolls back victim; restarts automatically
