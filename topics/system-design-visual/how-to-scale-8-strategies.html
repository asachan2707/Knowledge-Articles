How to Scale — 8 Strategies

Stateless services, caching, sharding, queues and more: eight ways to grow capacity.

2 min read · topic 9 of 9 in Infrastructure
  1. Stateless Services — no server-specific state; scale horizontally with ease
  2. Horizontal Scaling — add more servers; load balancer distributes
  3. Load Balancing — round robin, least connections for even distribution
  4. Auto Scaling — policies scale resources based on real-time traffic (AWS ASG)
  5. Caching — Redis for hot reads; CDN for static assets
  6. Database Replication — read replicas for scale + redundancy
  7. Database Sharding — partition data to scale writes
  8. Async Processing — move heavy tasks to queues/workers (Kafka, SQS)
