A prompt shortcut is a short, repeatable command phrase that bends ChatGPT's output toward a specific mode — simpler, more structured, more analytical, more creative. They work because they shift how the model frames its answer, not just what it answers.

How to use this guide: Each card shows the shortcut command, what it does to the model's output (visualized), and a real example you can copy. Stack multiple shortcuts together for compound effects — see the Power Combos section at the end.

Category 01 — Simplify & Explain

Simplify & Explain

These shortcuts tell ChatGPT to reduce complexity — turning dense, technical responses into something a specific audience can immediately grasp.

ELI5

Explain Like I'm 5

Strips away all jargon and reframes the concept using everyday objects, simple analogies, and vocabulary a child could follow. The model will find the simplest possible mental model for the idea.

ELI5 how TCP/IP works
TL;DR

Too Long; Didn't Read

Collapses any amount of content into its core essence — typically 2–4 bullets or a single crisp paragraph. Useful when you've pasted a long article or document and need the spine of it immediately.

TL;DR [paste article text here]
Give me an analogy for...

Analogy Unlock

Maps an unfamiliar technical concept onto something the reader already deeply understands. ChatGPT finds the structural similarity between the new idea and a known domain, making abstract concepts click instantly.

Give me an analogy for how DNS resolution works
Summarize this in X bullets

Bullet Summary

Forces the model to distill sprawling content into a fixed number of structured bullets. Specifying the count matters — "3 bullets" produces executive-summary density, "10 bullets" retains more nuance. The model has to decide what's worth keeping.

Summarize this meeting transcript in 5 bullets [paste text]
Category 02 — Role & Persona

Role & Persona

Assigning a role reshapes not just tone but knowledge depth, vocabulary, assumptions, and the frameworks the model applies. It changes the lens, not just the voice.

Act as a [role]

Expert Role Assignment

Tells ChatGPT to adopt the perspective, vocabulary, mental models, and professional assumptions of a specific expert. A doctor considers risks differently than a lawyer or a financial analyst — same question, radically different framing.

Act as a senior security engineer and review this API design for vulnerabilities
Explain this to a [audience]

Audience Calibration

Unlike ELI5 (which targets maximum simplicity), audience calibration sets a precise knowledge level — 10-year-old, high school student, domain expert, non-technical manager. The model tunes vocabulary, assumed background knowledge, and the depth of explanation accordingly.

Explain Kubernetes to a product manager who understands software but not infrastructure
Act as my interviewer for [role]

Mock Interview Mode

Flips ChatGPT from answerer to questioner. It will ask realistic interview questions one at a time, respond to your answers with follow-ups, and simulate the actual back-and-forth of a technical or behavioral interview — including challenging your weak answers.

Act as my interviewer for a senior backend engineer role at a fintech company. Ask me one question at a time.
Play devil's advocate

Devil's Advocate

Forces ChatGPT to argue the opposite of whatever you've proposed or whatever seems obvious. This is not about being contrarian — it's stress-testing your idea by surfacing the strongest version of the counter-argument before you commit to a decision.

Play devil's advocate: we're planning to rewrite our monolith in microservices. What's the strongest case against doing this?
Category 03 — Format & Structure

Format & Structure

ChatGPT's default output is prose. These shortcuts force it into a specific container — tables, steps, checklists, or code — which changes not just presentation but how deeply the model organizes its thinking.

Step by step

Sequential Breakdown

Transforms a single complex question into an ordered numbered walkthrough. Crucial for processes, debugging, tutorials, and any task where order matters. It also forces ChatGPT to think sequentially rather than dumping all information in parallel, often revealing assumptions it would otherwise skip.

Walk me through step by step how to set up Redis caching in a Node.js API
Format this as a table

Table Conversion

Restructures comparative or multi-attribute information into rows and columns. Comparing tools, frameworks, options, or features becomes instantly scannable. The model will infer the right column structure unless you specify it, making it great for rapid comparison even when you haven't thought through the dimensions yet.

Compare PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis in a table across: use case, consistency model, performance, and when not to use
Give me a checklist for...

Actionable Checklist

Turns conceptual advice into discrete, actionable checkbox items. Every item should be concrete enough that you can answer "done / not done" without interpretation. Great for pre-launch reviews, deployment procedures, onboarding tasks, or any process that needs to be verified step-by-step.

Give me a checklist for launching a production API — security, performance, observability, and reliability
Write this in [language] with comments

Code with Explanation

Specifying the language and requesting inline comments produces production-ready code with reasoning embedded. The comments reveal the model's intent at each step, making the output immediately reviewable — you can spot wrong assumptions before the code runs.

Write a Redis rate limiter in Node.js with inline comments explaining each decision
Category 04 — Analysis & Thinking

Analysis & Thinking

These commands unlock structured reasoning — forcing the model to organize its thinking rather than free-associate an answer.

List the pros and cons of...

Balanced Trade-off Analysis

Forces ChatGPT to populate both sides of a decision before drawing a conclusion. Left to its own, it often defaults to the expected answer. A pros/cons frame requires it to construct the strongest case on each side, surface non-obvious tradeoffs, and let you weigh them yourself.

List the pros and cons of moving from a monolith to microservices for a 15-person engineering team
What are the risks of...

Risk Surface Mapping

Asks ChatGPT to enumerate failure modes, edge cases, and second-order consequences. It will surface risks you haven't thought of — operational, security, business, and technical. Combine with "rank these by severity and likelihood" to get a prioritized risk register.

What are the risks of using JWT for auth in a distributed microservices system? Rank by severity.
Think step by step

Chain-of-Thought Trigger

This is one of the most powerful prompting techniques discovered in LLM research. Adding "think step by step" before a complex problem activates chain-of-thought reasoning, dramatically improving accuracy on math, logic, planning, and multi-step problems. The model externalizes its reasoning rather than pattern-matching to a conclusion.

Think step by step: if we shard our user table by user_id mod 8, what happens when we need to add a 9th shard?
Compare X vs Y for [context]

Contextual Comparison

The "for [context]" part is what makes this shortcut powerful — without it, you get a generic feature list. With context (your team size, use case, constraints), ChatGPT surfaces the differences that actually matter for your decision. It will also flag what both share so you focus only on where they diverge.

Compare Kafka vs RabbitMQ for a startup with 5 engineers building a real-time order pipeline
Category 05 — Create & Rewrite

Create & Rewrite

Commands that direct ChatGPT to generate new content or transform existing content — changing tone, density, or audience without changing meaning.

Brainstorm [N] ideas for...

Divergent Ideation

Specifying a number forces ChatGPT to exhaust the obvious answers and push into unconventional territory. Ask for 3 and you get the safe standard ideas. Ask for 15 and by idea 8 it's generating genuinely surprising options. The number is a creativity lever — higher numbers break the model out of first-instinct answers.

Brainstorm 12 ways a SaaS product could reduce churn without changing pricing
Rewrite this to be [tone/style]

Tone & Style Transformer

Preserves the meaning while changing the register: formal, casual, assertive, empathetic, concise, detailed, persuasive, or for a specific medium (Slack message, executive email, technical doc, tweet). The model treats the original as a content specification and rebuilds the expression layer from scratch.

Rewrite this Slack message as a formal executive summary email: [paste message]
Continue this

Content Continuation

Extends whatever you've started — code, a document, a story, a list — maintaining the exact style, format, and voice of the original. ChatGPT infers structure, terminology, and tone from what you've provided and carries it forward seamlessly. Works best when you provide a substantial sample (100+ words) to lock in the style.

Continue this technical blog post, maintaining the same conversational tone and code examples: [paste text]
Improve this / Make this better

Quality Elevator

Without further instruction, ChatGPT will improve clarity, conciseness, grammar, flow, and structure. Add a specific dimension for sharper results: "improve the clarity", "make this more persuasive", "improve the technical accuracy". It will also explain what it changed if you ask — which is its own learning tool.

Improve this README — make it clearer for a new developer joining the project: [paste README]
Category 06 — Precision & Control

Precision & Control

Modifiers that tighten ChatGPT's output to exactly what you need — no waffle, no padding, no assumptions about your knowledge level.

In one sentence...

Forced Concision

The hardest thing to do well is to explain something complex in a single sentence — and ChatGPT has to find the most signal-dense framing possible. Use this to test if you actually understand a concept (if ChatGPT can summarize it in one sentence, you should be able to too), or for elevator pitches, taglines, and micro-documentation.

In one sentence, explain what the CAP theorem means for a product manager
Ask me clarifying questions before answering

Precision Interviewer

Prevents ChatGPT from assuming what you meant and producing a generic answer. It will instead ask 3–5 targeted questions to understand your actual context — team size, constraints, goals, prior attempts. The answer you get after answering the questions is far more specific and actionable than anything it would produce from the original vague prompt.

Ask me clarifying questions before answering: I need to improve my system's performance.
Give me 3 real-world examples of...

Concept Concretizer

Forces abstract concepts to land in reality. "3 real-world examples" gets you case studies, company stories, or production scenarios. The number matters — 3 gives you breadth across different contexts; 1 gives you depth on the best single case. Always specify "real-world" to prevent generic/fictional examples.

Give me 3 real-world examples of companies that used the Strangler Fig pattern to migrate from a monolith
What am I missing?

Blind Spot Detector

The most underused shortcut. After presenting a plan, design, or decision, asking "what am I missing?" activates ChatGPT as a reviewer rather than a generator. It will look for unstated assumptions, unconsidered edge cases, gaps in logic, and risks you haven't named. Follow up with "what's the single most important thing to fix first?"

Here's my system design for a payment API [describe it]. What am I missing? Focus on reliability and security gaps.
Category 07 — Power Combos

Power Combos

Individual shortcuts are useful. Stacked, they become prompts that consistently produce senior-quality output from a junior-level question. These are the patterns that separate power users from casual users.

Combo What it produces Example
Act as [expert] + Think step by step Expert-level sequential reasoning — deep assumptions + structured logic Act as a distributed systems architect. Think step by step: how would you design a rate limiter for 1M req/s?
Ask clarifying questions + Give examples Eliminates ambiguity first, then makes the answer concrete Ask me 3 clarifying questions, then give 3 real-world examples of the pattern I should use.
Pros/cons + Devil's advocate Full decision-grade analysis — structured then stress-tested Give me the pros and cons of GraphQL, then play devil's advocate on the top pro.
ELI5 → then go deeper Progressive complexity — builds intuition before adding nuance ELI5 consistent hashing. Now explain it as if I'm a senior engineer who already uses hash tables.
What am I missing + Rank by impact Blind spot detection + prioritized action list Here's my architecture. What am I missing? Rank the gaps by business impact.
In a table + Add a recommendation column Scannable comparison that ends with a decision, not just data Compare these 4 databases in a table. Add a "When to choose this" column as the last column.

The Meta-Pattern

Every powerful prompt has three elements: (1) Context — who you are, what system you're working on, what constraints exist. (2) Command — the shortcut that shapes the mode of response. (3) Output spec — how many items, what format, what detail level. Most prompts fail because they skip context and output spec. The shortcut alone is only 1/3 of the formula.

Prefix your prompt with one or more of these to instantly lock the visual style, genre, lighting, environment, character type, or post-processing treatment. Key Takeaways

For text prompts (Part 1): Shortcuts are not magic — they are frames that shift the model's reasoning mode. The most powerful ones (think step by step, ask clarifying questions, what am I missing) activate a different cognitive process entirely. Master 5–6 deeply and stack them — you'll consistently outperform someone who knows 50 prompts but uses each in isolation.

For image shortcuts (Part 2): Every shortcut you add is a style directive that compounds with the others. Don't think of them as filters — think of them as creative briefs. Two shortcuts define a genre; four or five define a production quality and purpose. The stacking combos table gives you the fastest path to professional-grade AI images for any use case.
